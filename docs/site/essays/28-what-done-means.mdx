title: 'What "done" means: deployed, but not yet deciding anything'
description: "Five meanings of done, and the three public records that keep Parmana honest about which one each feature has reached: the claims register, the gap log and the remaining work list."
---

In October 2026 Parmana shipped business validation: authority grants with limits, facts checked against the system that
owns them, and re-checks just before release. It is merged, deployed with its migrations, released in SDK 1.6.0, and it
passed live checks in production.

It does not decide a single real action yet. That is not a contradiction; it is the most important distinction in the
project, and most announcements blur it.

## Five meanings of done

1. **Built:** the code exists and its tests pass.
2. **Deployed:** it runs in production.
3. **Verified in production:** someone exercised it on the live system and saw it behave.
4. **In use:** a real policy turns it on, so it affects real decisions.
5. **Proven on real actions:** a real action went through it, and the record exists.

Business validation is at step 3. No shipped policy requires a grant or declares a source, and no real business system
is connected; the live check used a demo source. Until the refund policy is changed, through the same two-person
approval as everything else, real refunds are decided as before.

"Parmana now verifies refund eligibility with your order system" could be defended word by word, and the impression
would still be false.

## Three public records

Three files sit in the repository, next to the code.

**The claims register (`docs/CLAIMS.md`).** Every claim with its scope and the files and tests that back it. Claims are
corrected in place, with a date, when they turn out broader than the evidence. It ends with "Claims we intentionally do
not make": unscoped "non-bypassable", "tamper-proof" in every environment, guaranteed compliance. No implementation could
back those, so they are excluded on principle.

**The gap log (`docs/VERIFICATION-GAPS.md`).** Every time something is found missing or wrong, it gets an id, a
severity, a date and, later, how and when it was closed. It holds close to ninety entries.

One example shows why. It was found that in one deployment path the refund agent service could call the payment provider
itself after Parmana had already released the refund, so an approved refund could have been paid twice. It went into the
log, was fixed and deployed the next day, and a refusal through that path was verified in production. Even so, the log
still marks "an approved refund observed live making exactly one payment call" as **not claimed**, because only tests
cover it.

**The remaining work list (`docs/REMAINING-WORK.md`).** What is left, who has to do it, and in what order. For business
validation it lists operator steps (one real refund end to end, connect the system that owns refund eligibility, the
first real grant, switch the refund policy, one real refund under it), evidence not yet collected, what is not built
(cumulative limits, built-in connectors re-asking at release, limiting which facts a source may answer) and open
questions. The public version is [What is left](/concepts/business-validation#what-is-left).

## Why bother

- **The product is trust.** Parmana exists so someone else can check, without trusting the operator, what an agent was
  allowed to do and why. Documentation that must be taken on faith would undermine that.
- **Security reviews find gaps anyway.** It is better they find them in a dated list with fixes.
- **It prevents self-deception.** "Deployed" feels like "done"; writing "not yet in use" next to it stops moving on too
  early.
- **Progress becomes visible.** Each move from not built to verified to in use is a real step with evidence.

## The status words

Every feature on this site carries a label such as **[AVAILABLE]**, **[REQUIRED]**, **[NOT AVAILABLE]** or
**[ROADMAP]**, and a "partially" is always followed by exactly which part. A page that says "no real business system
connected yet" is not exciting. When it changes to "connected, and here is the record", it will mean something.

## Try it

For one week, write next to every feature which of the five meanings of done it has reached, and keep a list of the
claims you will not make.
