## What it is

A connector performs the action on a real system once Parmana has released it. Parmana decides whether an action may run. The connector does the work, and it is the only part that touches the external system.

## What Parmana ships

Parmana ships no built-in connectors. The connectors that were once built into this repository moved out of it under [ADR-0018](https://github.com/pavancharak/parmana-core/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-0018-Connectors-Outside-Core.md).

To connect a system, an operator registers an external connector: a capability, the endpoint that serves it, and the policy that governs it. The registration goes through maker and checker approval, with no deploy ([ADR-0013](https://github.com/pavancharak/parmana-core/blob/main/docs/adr/ADR-0013-Generic-External-Connector.md)).

See the [Connector Development Guide](/integrations/connector-development-guide) for how to write one.
