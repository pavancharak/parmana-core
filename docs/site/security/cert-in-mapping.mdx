CERT-In, India's national cybersecurity agency, has proposed a draft framework of Artificial Intelligence Security
Auditing Guidelines. The draft classifies AI systems by risk (low, moderate, high and critical), puts particular
weight on AI agents, and asks for least privilege, a kill switch for high-risk agentic systems, continuous
monitoring and independent audits for critical systems, and reporting of AI security incidents within six hours.

This page maps Parmana to the provisions of that draft as reported. It is a draft, so the final text may differ.
Read the source text from CERT-In before relying on any row. This page is a mapping, not a certification or legal
opinion.

**What Parmana is, for these purposes.** Parmana is not an AI model. It sits between an AI agent and the systems it
acts on, refuses any action that a registered person has not approved under a versioned policy, and signs a record
of each approved action and each refusal. It covers actions that go through its gateway. It does not see inside the
model, the training data or the content the model produces.

Ratings, as on the [regulation mapping](/security/regulation-mapping):

- **Supports:** Parmana provides a technical measure for the requirement, for actions that go through it, with
  evidence you can check.
- **Partly:** Parmana provides part of it. The rest is named.
- **Organizational:** the requirement is about people, process or documentation. Parmana can record it, not do it.
- **Not covered:** the requirement is outside what Parmana does.

## Open items that matter here

- **Direct agent paths.** An agent that reaches a system without going through Parmana is outside the controls above
  (see [Limitations](/security/limitations)).
- **Approver scope.** Approvers are not yet limited to particular actions or policies.
- **Signed status.** The kill switch status and audit events are not yet signed.
- **Independent assurance.** No third-party audit has been completed.

## Reading this page

- **Only actions that go through Parmana are covered.** Give agents credentials only to Parmana, so it is the one route
  to your systems.
- **Each rating has evidence you can check.** Claim numbers refer to the [claims register](https://github.com/pavancharak/parmana-core/blob/main/docs/internal/CLAIMS.md).
- **The draft may change.** Check the final CERT-In text and your system's risk class with counsel.
