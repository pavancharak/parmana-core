---
title: "Interface: RetryPolicy"
---

Immutable retry configuration.

## Properties

### enabled?

> `readonly` `optional` **enabled?**: `boolean`

Enables or disables retries.

Default:
false

***

### maxAttempts?

> `readonly` `optional` **maxAttempts?**: `number`

Maximum retry attempts.

Default:
0

***

### initialDelayMs?

> `readonly` `optional` **initialDelayMs?**: `number`

Initial retry delay in milliseconds.

Default:
1000

***

### maxDelayMs?

> `readonly` `optional` **maxDelayMs?**: `number`

Maximum delay between retries.

Default:
30000

***

### strategy?

> `readonly` `optional` **strategy?**: [`RetryStrategy`](/sdks/reference/typescript/enumerations/RetryStrategy)

Retry backoff strategy.

Default:
EXPONENTIAL
