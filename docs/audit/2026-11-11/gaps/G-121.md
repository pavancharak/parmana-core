Checked against the published packages, not only the source. The published `@parmana/sdk@2.0.2` (npm) and `parmana@2.0.1` (PyPI) both verify a schema version 3 hybrid record from parmana-core at `7e9a54fb`, and both reject the same record after `updatedAt` is changed.
