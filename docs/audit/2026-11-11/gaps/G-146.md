No business system calls the inbound check, and no probe checks that a direct call is refused.** The check exists, but nothing in production sends it a request, and no scheduled job sends a direct request to a business system and expects a refusal. It is not scheduled, and no business system is configured as a target, so the gap stays open until a business system owner configures a target and the probe runs on a schedule. It is the working part of G-129. Closing it: a reference business system or client that calls the check, and a scheduled probe, recorded as a signed record, that reports "not verified" when it cannot run. Process: linked to G-129 and issue #421; no separate issue yet.
