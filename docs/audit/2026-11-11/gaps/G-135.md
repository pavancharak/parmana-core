## Exact entry text

**G-135. Approvals can be rubber-stamped under volume.** Every write needs a signed human approval, but nothing limits how many approvals a reviewer sees or how fast they must decide. Under volume the approval becomes a formality. The DSCI discussion paper on AI liability for agentic AI (2026, condition 05) names this failure mode. Status: open. Closing it needs approval batching or thresholds for low-risk actions, and a measure of approval volume and decision time per approver. Process: linked issue #427. Claim: CLAIMS 2.47 (approval before action); this gap limits how far that holds under load.
