## Exact entry text

**G-150. CLAIMS.md section 5 says nothing notifies anyone on refusal, but the approval notifier does.** Section 5 says a refused decision does not escalate and that "nothing notifies anyone". `RuntimeEngine.decide` calls `notifyApprovalNeeded` when an approval would cure a refusal. `packages/api/src/bootstrap/createApprovalNeededNotifier.ts` sends `approval.needed` events to an operator-configured webhook (`APPROVAL_WEBHOOK_URL`) or email (`APPROVAL_EMAIL_TO`). With neither configured, nothing is sent. Delivery is best effort and is not retried, and the request stays refused. Status: closed. The not-claimed bullet in `CLAIMS.md` now describes the approval notifier, its settings, its best-effort delivery and that it is not retried (claim 2.46), and the changelog records the correction. Process: linked issue #454.
