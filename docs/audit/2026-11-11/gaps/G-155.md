## Exact entry text

**G-155. No review-and-challenge flow for AI decisions (contestability).** Parmana records approvals and refusals, but gives no defined way for a person to challenge, correct or override an AI decision after it is made, and no analysis of override or agreement rates. CERT-In AI Security Auditing Guidelines v1.0 section 11 (Table 25 and Table 75) requires review criteria, reviewer information, escalation, reviewer independence and override analysis, and section 8.8 requires override logging. Status: open. Opportunity: a challenge record linked to the original signed decision, routed to a named reviewer, with override and agreement rates reported. Overlaps G-65 and G-135, but the challenge-after-decision question is new. Process: linked issue #465.
