**G-154. No AI bill of materials (AIBOM) or model-artifact integrity check.** Parmana does not produce an AI bill of materials for the systems it governs, and does not verify model or artifact hashes before use. CERT-In AI Security Auditing Guidelines v1.0 requires an AIBOM for production systems (section 8.3), hash validation of model artifacts (Table 101) and integrity checks before loading (section 10.3.1). Status: open. Opportunity: let a customer register an AIBOM for each agent or policy set, store its hash in the signed record of each approval, and refuse an action whose recorded hash does not match the registered one.
