## Exact entry text

**G-50. Policy approver authority is not scoped. Any provisioned human checker can approve any
policy.** Found 2026-09-20. `POST /policies/pending-changes/:id/approve`
(`packages/api/src/routes/pending-policy-changes.ts`) enforces a human credential
(`credentialHolderType === USER`, `isHumanCaller.ts`), that the checker is not the proposer, and a valid
single use step up signature. It does not ask whether this checker may approve this particular policy.
`PolicyChangeApprovalService` records `approvedBy` as the API `callerId`, not an `Authority`. Two human
keys with step up keys can therefore approve a change to the refund limit or a payment policy. There is
also no quorum, no separation by policy sensitivity, and no role check. `credentialHolderType` is
operator provisioned configuration, not proof that a business authority approved. This is the reason the
execution claim is stated as "matches what a human checker approved" and not "within business authority
bounds". **Partly fixed 2026-10-20, for policy changes.** The approve and reject routes now check the caller against `POLICY_CHANGE_CHECKERS`, a per policy list of caller ids. A caller not listed for the policy gets `403 CHECKER_NOT_IN_SCOPE`. It fails closed when the variable is unset, so no one can approve or reject until it is configured. Reject was added after approve, in the same change set as the runbooks; before that, reject checked only the human caller, the maker and the step-up signature. Tests: `packages/api/tests/unit/policyChangeCheckers.test.ts` and the approve tests in `pending-policy-changes-governance.integration.test.ts`. **Still open:** approval issuers (signed approval artifacts) are not scoped per policy or action; there is no quorum; the scope is a configured list of caller ids, not a role; `PolicyChangeApprovalRecord` still records the caller id, not a resolved principal.

**Update 2026-10-21.** The production entry (`api/index.ts`) did not pass `POLICY_CHANGE_CHECKERS`, so no approval could succeed there. That fault is G-123, fixed in `4e79d57`. A live approval by a listed checker then succeeded on production (CLAIMS 2.59). The approval issuer scope and the lack of quorum are unchanged.
