**G-129. Callers (AI agents, applications or people) that reach your systems directly are not checked or recorded by Parmana.** Parmana checks and records actions that leave through its gateway. An agent run by a customer or a partner that calls one of your systems directly never reaches Parmana: no policy decides that call, no approval is required, and no Trust Record is written. The README's outbound rules (a business system never calls Parmana, and a caller never calls a connector directly) protect the path Parmana controls and do not cover this one. Status: open. Decided 2026-11-05 (RFC-0024, final): option A is the design, and an inbound agent must not reach a business system without Parmana's check. Done: the limit is documented on the public limitations page (option C, as an interim measure); the check endpoint `POST /v1/inbound/checks` exists and is tested (claim 3.30), but no business system calls it, and, since G-142 (pull request #448), it evaluates the capability's policy. Not done: the no-bypass probe (G-146), the policy name and version on each record (G-143), and the permanent source of facts (G-144, RFC-0026, draft). Not checked: how often inbound agents already call business systems in production. Tests: `packages/api/tests/unit/routes/inbound-checks.test.ts`, ten tests. Process: linked issue #421 (P1); RFC-0024 (decided), RFC-0026 (draft). Claim: README and the public limitations page state the limit; claim 3.30 covers the check.
