Industries

Where AI acts with your money, your claims or your records.

Regulated businesses already define who may approve what. Parmana applies those same limits to AI agents, and keeps a record of each decision.

Banking

Payments, limits and account changes

An agent may prepare a transfer, but only a named approver can release one above your limit. Account detail changes follow the same rule. Each decision is recorded.

Insurance

Claims and settlements

An agent may triage a claim and propose a settlement within the amount your claims authority allows. Exceptions go to a person, and the approval is kept with the claim.

Payments

Refunds, payouts and reversals

Refunds below your threshold run automatically. Anything above it needs a signed approval for that order and amount, usable once.

What changes for your team

Authority is written down

Limits and approval rules are versioned, so you can show what applied on a given date.

Refusals are evidence too

An action that was refused is recorded with the rule that refused it, not dropped from the logs.

Auditors check without system access

A signed record can be verified offline with the public key you choose to trust.

Changes to rules are reviewed

Policy changes go through a second person, and each change is signed.

These are illustrations of how Parmana could be configured, not customer deployments. Parmana is a prototype. Check what is built and tested today before you plan around any of these uses.
