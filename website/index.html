How it works

Each action passes through four stages before it runs. Each stage is built and tested. Parmana is open source under the Apache License 2.0.

DeclaredYour institution writes its authority as explicit, versioned rules: what each action may do, up to what amount, and when a person must approve.
AuthorizedEach proposed action is checked against those rules. If no rule matches, the action is refused.
ExecutedOnly permitted actions pass the enforcement boundary. On supported paths the agent never holds the connector credentials.
ProvenA signed record of the decision and the release, which anyone can verify offline with the public key they choose to trust.

An example

A support agent asks to refund 4,000 to a customer.

Your policy lets the agent refund up to 500 on its own. Above 500, it needs a signed approval from a named manager for that order and that amount.

Parmana refuses the request, so nothing is refunded. The manager reviews it and signs an approval for that order and amount, valid for 15 minutes and usable once. The agent sends the request again. Parmana releases the refund, and the record shows the decision, the approval and the release.

What makes this important

Parmana does not record what an AI agent says it did. It checks authorization independently and binds that decision to evidence of what actually executed.

Keep control when AI acts for your business. Parmana shows whether every AI action stayed within the authority you gave it, and gives you evidence you can check.

How it compares

Parmana sits between an agent's request and the system it would change. It is not a monitoring dashboard, a prompt filter or an access-control list.

Access controlsDecide whether an agent can reach a system. They do not decide whether a specific action was delegated.
Logs and monitoringRecord what the agent reports, or what the system saw. Parmana checks the action against your rules before it runs.
ParmanaChecks each action against authority your institution defined, and produces signed evidence of the decision and the release.
