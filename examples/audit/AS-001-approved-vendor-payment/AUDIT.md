# Executive Summary

This audit report documents the successful execution of a Vendor Payment transaction using Parmana.

The transaction was authorized by a human authority, evaluated against the Vendor Payment Policy, executed by the Runtime, independently verified, deterministically replayed, and issued a cryptographic receipt.

No policy violations were detected.

---

# Audit Objective

Demonstrate that the transaction:

- was initiated by a legitimate authority
- was explicitly authorized
- matched an approved business intent
- complied with the applicable policy
- executed successfully
- produced immutable execution evidence
- can be independently verified
- can be deterministically replayed
- produced a cryptographic receipt
